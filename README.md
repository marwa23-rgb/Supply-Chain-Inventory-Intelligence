## 🎯 Business Challenge
Improve visibility into inventory performance, supplier quality, lead times, shipping costs, and transportation efficiency to support better replenishment and sourcing decisions.

## ⚡ What I Built
- Prepared SKU-level supply chain data
- Validated data quality and consistency
- Analyzed inventory, sales, suppliers, transportation, and routes
- Engineered stock coverage and performance KPIs
- Used SQL aggregations and window functions
- Designed a Power BI dashboard structure for supply chain monitoring

## 📊 Key KPIs
| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total SKUs | Number of unique products |
| Total Units Sold | Overall product demand |
| Total Revenue | Revenue generated across products |
| Stock Coverage | Stock level relative to product demand |
| Average Defect Rate | Product and supplier quality indicator |
| Average Lead Time | Supplier performance indicator |
| Shipping Cost | Transportation efficiency indicator |

## 💡 Business Impact
This analysis helps decision-makers:
- Prioritize replenishment for low-stock and high-demand SKUs
- Monitor suppliers with higher defect rates
- Compare transportation modes by cost, time, and quality
- Identify efficient shipping routes
- Improve sourcing and inventory decisions
